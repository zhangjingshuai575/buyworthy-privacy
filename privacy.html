BuyWorthy — Privacy Policy

Effective date:

BuyWorthy is a browser extension that analyzes product pages on supported shopping sites and gives you a clear “worth buying” score. This policy explains what data, if any, the extension handles, and how. The short version: everything runs locally in your own browser — nothing is collected, uploaded, or stored about you.

1. What BuyWorthy does

When you open a product page on a supported site (for example Amazon or JD.com) and start an analysis, the extension reads the visible text of that page — such as the product name, price, and customer reviews — and computes a score and a few plain-language reasons.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • No personal information — we do not read or collect your name, email, address, account, or identity.
  • No browsing history — we do not track which pages you visit or build a history of your activity.
  • No uploads — no data is sent to us. BuyWorthy has no server, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • No third-party sharing — because nothing is collected, nothing is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

3. How the analysis happens

All processing happens entirely inside your own browser, on your device. The page text is read in memory only, the score is computed locally, and the result is shown to you in a side panel. The moment you close the page, none of that information persists.

4. Local storage

The extension uses the browser’s storage permission only for small, non-personal convenience settings (if any) — for example a remembered preference. This data lives only in your browser’s local extension storage and never leaves your device. Removing the extension deletes it.

5. Permissions, explained

  • activeTab — lets the extension read the current page only when you click the button, so it can analyze the product you asked about.
  • storage — used for the small local preferences described above.
  • Site access (amazon.com, jd.com) — limited to the shopping sites this tool is designed to work on.

6. Children’s privacy

BuyWorthy is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the support contact listed on the extension’s Chrome Web Store listing.
